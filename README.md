# maat

**Personal planning on top of Apple Calendar and Reminders.**

maat brings your events, tasks, and deadlines together in one place. Instead of switching between Calendar and Reminders, you see everything side by side, link what belongs together, and plan your days and weeks the way you like to work.

*Why?*

Apple Calendar and Reminders are great on their own, but they keep things apart: appointments in one app, to-dos in the other. Real planning needs both at once. maat doesn't replace either app. It adds a shared layer on top, so your plans fit your available time, deadlines, and priorities.
